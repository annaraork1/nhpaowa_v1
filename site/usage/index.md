# Basic Usage

## Sign in

Open the app and sign in with your association email and password. Your role decides what you see — there is no role picker.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/login.png" alt="Sign-in page" />

The sign-in page also links to the login-free [flat self-update](/usage/flat-self-update) channels, so residents without an account can update their details.

Sessions last 7 days. Use **Sign out** in the top-right corner on shared desks.

## Home

After sign-in you land on **Home**: one card per screen you're allowed to use, with a short description. Tap a card to open it. Residents get a separate [resident home](/usage/resident-portal).

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/home.png" alt="Operations home" />

## Layout

| Area | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| Left menu | Every screen your role can open. On phones it collapses behind the ☰ button |
| Search box | Global search across items, suppliers, purchase orders, and more (staff roles) |
| Bell | Alerts and notifications — low stock, AMC expiry, bus gate exceptions. Mark them read here or on **Alerts** |
| Moon / sun | Switch between light and dark themes. The choice is remembered on that device |
| Your name | Shows your role |

## Common patterns

**Row actions.** Lists show icon buttons with tooltips: 👁 view, ✏️ edit, 🗑 delete. You only see the actions your role allows. Deletes always ask for confirmation, and most records are archived rather than erased so history is preserved.

**Search and filters.** Long lists (flats, items, transactions) have a search box plus dropdown filters. The result count updates as you type; **Clear filters** resets them.

**CSV and PDF.** Most registers offer **Export CSV**, a **CSV template**, and **Import CSV**. Official documents — NOCs, notices, move gate passes, reports — download as PDFs on the association letterhead.

**Phones.** On narrow screens, tables turn into stacked cards with the same actions. See [Mobile & PWA](/usage/mobile).
