# NOC Certificates

The association issues No-Objection Certificates to flat owners for sale, loans, rentals, and renovations. **NOC** tracks each request from submission to an issued, printable letter.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/noc.png" alt="NOC certificates" />

## Purposes

- Sale / transfer
- Bank loan / mortgage
- Rental / tenant
- Renovation
- Other

## Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Submitted | Request raised by staff or a resident |
| Issued | Approved, with issue date and validity notes |
| Rejected | Declined with a reason |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn |

1. Select **New NOC request**, choose the flat, purpose, applicant details, and notes.
2. Attach supporting documents (📎) if needed.
3. An approver opens the request and selects **Issue** or **Reject**.
4. Print (🖨) the issued NOC. The letter carries the association letterhead, the board list, the flat and owner details, the no-objection statement, the date, and the issuer.

Filter the list by status with the dropdown at the top. A flat's NOC history is available from its record.

## Who can do what

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Superadmin, Secretary, Treasurer | Create, issue, reject |
| Committee, Security Head, Estate Manager | View |
| Resident | Request NOCs for their own flats and track status |
