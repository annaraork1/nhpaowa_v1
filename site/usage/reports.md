# Reports & Alerts

## Reports

Pick a date range, then export any report as CSV or PDF.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/reports.png" alt="Reports" />

| Report | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| Stock valuation | Current value of each item (stock × unit price), by category |
| Consumption | Quantities issued per item in the period |
| Supplier spend | Purchase order totals per supplier in the period |

Other modules have their own exports:

| Module | Exports |
|--------|---------|
| Flat owners | Register CSV and PDF |
| Attendance | Payroll CSV, clock CSV |
| Bus reports | Daily CSV/PDF, reconciliation, wait times, congestion |
| Notices, NOC, moves | PDF documents |

## Alerts

The bell in the top bar and the **Alerts** page collect everything that needs attention.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/alerts.png" alt="Alerts" />

| Alert | Raised when |
|-------|-------------|
| Low stock | An item reaches its minimum level |
| PO due | A purchase order passes its expected date |
| AMC expiry | A contract is within its lead time of expiring |
| Stock received | Goods are received against a purchase order |
| New supplier | A supplier is added |
| Notice published | A resident notice goes live |
| Bus gate alerts | Long wait, off-slot, unmatched trip, batch capacity, AM/PM mismatch |

Alerts carry a severity (info, warning, critical). Select **Mark read** to clear one.

## Dashboard

The [inventory dashboard](/usage/inventory#dashboard) summarises stock value, pending purchase orders, low stock, top consumption, and recent alerts.
