# Resident Portal

Residents with an account get their own, simpler experience — not the operations menus.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/resident-home.png" alt="Resident home" />

## Home

- **My flats** — every flat linked to the account, with type and occupancy, and a shortcut to the household.
- Counts of open NOCs, active moves, and published notices.
- Quick links: My flat & household, Request NOC, Request move, Notices, Today's buses.

## What residents can do

| Screen | Resident can |
|--------|--------------|
| **My flat** | View members and household; submit changes to vehicles, pets, parking, emergency contacts, and access devices. Changes wait for staff approval |
| **NOC** | Request a NOC for their own flat and follow its status |
| **Moves** | Request a move-in, move-out, or internal shift for their own flat |
| **Notices** | Read and download published notices |
| **Today's buses** | See today's school bus schedule and status |

Residents can't see other flats, approve anything, or open operations screens. The API enforces the same limits.

## Getting an account

The Superadmin creates the account with the **Resident** role, and staff link it to the flat as a member on **Flat owners**. Try it with the demo account `resident@nhpaowa.local` (Tower 1 / 101).

Residents who don't want an account can still update their contact details through the [flat self-update link](/usage/flat-self-update).
