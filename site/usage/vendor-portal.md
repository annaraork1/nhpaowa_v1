# Vendor Portal

Suppliers can sign in to see the purchase orders the association has raised with them. The portal is read-only and shows **only that supplier's** orders.

<img class="screenshot" src="/screenshots/vendor-portal.png" alt="Vendor portal" />

## Setting up a vendor login

1. Make sure the supplier exists under **Suppliers**.
2. The Superadmin opens **Users → Add user**, chooses the **Vendor** role, and selects the supplier. Creating a vendor without a supplier is rejected.
3. Share the email and temporary password with the supplier.

Vendors land directly on the portal after sign-in and have no other menu items. Try it with the demo account `vendor@shree.local`.
