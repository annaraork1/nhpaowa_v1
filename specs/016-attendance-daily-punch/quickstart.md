# Quickstart: Daily Punch & Absent

1. Attendance → Roster.
2. **Check in** (labeled button) → shows In since ….
3. Immediate second click / early **Check out** → rejected until ≥30s; then Check out → **Done for today**.
4. Further Check in same day → error.
5. Another staff with no punch → **Mark absent** → Absent; **Clear absent** if they arrive, then Check in.
6. History / summary reflect Present / Absent / On leave.
