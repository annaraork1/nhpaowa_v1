# Quickstart: 021 Attendance Punch Detail

1. Restart inventory-service.
2. Attendance → Roster: Check in a staff member, wait 30s, Check out.
3. Confirm Today shows In time, Out time, and hours (e.g. `0.01 h`+).
4. History tab: PRESENT rows show Check in / Check out / Hours.
5. Mobile width: DONE card lists In / Out / Hours fields.
